About

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Geneva, working between the DMML group at HES-SO Genève and the Kinesiology Laboratory (UNIGE/HUG). I'm supervised by Alexandros Kalousis, Stéphane Marchand-Maillet and Stéphane Armand, as part of the SNSF-funded Metathesis project.

I'm also co-founder and CTO of a-gO, which builds 3D motion capture and movement analysis tools. I studied Life Science Engineering at EPFL.

Research

Human movement involves hundreds of muscles and dozens of joints, yet what we actually do with them is highly structured. I try to learn that structure and use it in two directions.

Control

Muscle synergies as a low-dimensional action space for reinforcement learning in physics-based musculoskeletal models. Many muscle activations, driven by a few shared patterns.

Clinical decision support

Modelling pathological gait to support treatment selection for children with cerebral palsy, as part of the Metathesis project.
